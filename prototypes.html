Valuation prototypes: audit and reuse

The earlier prototypes were inspected as sources. They and the installed Scatter applications were not modified or executed as part of this audit. This report distinguishes code inspection from live financial-data reconciliation.

1 · Economy notebooks

In Econ_simulator.ipynb, cells 4–8 construct P/S as MC/(P×Y), where P×Y is nominal GDP. Monetary_systems_lab.ipynb cell 6 does the same. That is market-cap/GDP, not corporate price/sales. P/E is separately MC/earnings, but its validity depends on the earnings definition and how MC was formed.

The prior nominal-capital accumulation, ambiguous credit/velocity terms, fiscal double-counting risk and denominator floors motivated the explicit stock-flow core. The original notebook accounting residual was not treated as evidence that its ledger closed. The new model reports goods, sector, bank and stock-change residuals at every run.

2 · Comps and trailing-ratio work

MM Comps 2.ipynb includes a strict quote/financial-currency conversion path for EV/EBITDA. It withholds missing-FX comparisons and nonpositive EBITDA ratios. Those safeguards inform the new issuer layer. The trailing_valuation_ratios_m.ipynb work constructs quarterly-to-TTM valuation series; a future observed-history integration should retain publication lags and period alignment, not only a rolling sum.

This audit read the relevant code and formula definitions; it did not prove current vendor coverage, TTM completeness, historical availability or the economic equivalence of all reported EBITDA definitions.

3 · Scatter accounting and enterprise-value bridge

The installed mm_financial_metrics.py and mm_accounting_lab.py separate debt, preferred capital, non-controlling interests, cash/short-term investments and non-operating investments. Unknown claims remain missing; reconstructing debt or liquid assets requires all necessary components. Operating EBITDA and financing claims require matched scope, and leases need explicit review.

The new issuer layer applies that discipline: explicit excess cash, preferred/NCI/pension/lease claims, one reporting currency, known FX and unavailable output when the bridge is incomplete. Long-term investments require classification as separable assets rather than automatic subtraction. Financial institutions require a different operating/financing model.

The ae_macro_engine.py prototype provides market/rate/carry/regime analytics. Its market scores are not a structural economy-to-cash-flow valuation model, so they have not been treated as proof of fair value here.

4 · What changed in this lab

  • GDP, corporate value added and sales have different labels and equations.
  • Private and public investment have explicit real-capital counterparts; dividends reconcile to FCFE.
  • Valuation separates operating flows, financing claims, reinvestment, risk-free rates and risk premia.
  • Corporate, land and bank income reaches the specified household or public owner.
  • Unknown claims, currency mismatches and unmatched capitalised leases are visible.
  • Historical inputs, assumptions and conditional projections remain separate.
