Implemented equations and boundaries

Version 2.0.0. Annual nominal flows; real production stocks; eight time steps per year by default. Symbols below describe the implemented closure. These equations are original modelling choices unless labelled identity. All rates are decimal annual rates in Python and percent where labelled in the interface.

1 · Institutions, units and accounting identities

Wage households, asset households, businesses, a consolidated bank, government and a foreign settlement counterpart. Business current and capital accounts are separate matrix columns. The bank distributes net income immediately, with zero bank equity in this closure. Net business debt Q is loans less business deposits, not a detailed gross bank-loan portfolio. Government bills are held by the model bank. There is no default, deposit insurance loss or reserve-constrained bank capital rule.

Y = Cw + Co + I + G + NX.
Wages = (1 − θ)Y; land rent = ρY; corporate EBITDA = θY − ρY.
Sector balances: Sw + So + (Sf − I) − deficit − NX = 0.
Bank identity: B + Q + H + N + R − T = Dw + Do.

Y is nominal value added. P starts at one. Q and H are business net debt and household debt; N is the bank’s net foreign settlement asset. D = Dw + Do are household deposits. R is locked reserve backing and T is Treasury credit to the bank. Corporate and land ownership marks are assets of their holders and claims on the issuer/property, not new net financial resources for the whole economy.

2 · Stocks and flows

ΔB = deficit × Δt; ΔQ = (I − Sf)Δt; ΔH = household borrowing × Δt.
ΔDw = (Sw + household borrowing)Δt; ΔDo = So Δt; ΔN = NX Δt.
R = backing × D; T = R − opening bill offset.

Each tax, rent, interest, dividend and transfer has a corresponding payer and recipient in the transaction matrix. Public dividends and public rents reduce the fiscal deficit. Bill cancellation is an opening offset against Treasury funding, not a gain in aggregate financial wealth. Positive net liquid wealth, rather than a future DCF mark, forms the model’s wealth-tax base. Treasury and reserve interest appear in both government and bank accounts. The model rejects funding that would require a negative T under this closure.

3 · Production and investment

Effective labour = population index × relative working-age share × relative participation × hours/40.
Capacity = normalised A × effective capital^α × effective labour^(1−α).
ΔK = [(1 − research share)I/P − δK]Δt.
ΔKnowledge = [research share × I/P − δKnowledge]Δt.
ΔJ = [public-investment fraction × G/P − δJ]Δt.

The capital and population scales are rebased to the reference opening calibration. Shorter paid hours or lower participation are not normalised away. Productivity growth includes an expected research spillover proportional to success × knowledge/(physical + knowledge capital). Failed research still costs resources and depreciates. Private depreciation covers both capital types; public depreciation is separate. Public investment is included in G once.

Desired investment rises with normal capacity and utilisation and falls with the assumed real loan-rate/natural-rate gap. Investment, wages’ distribution share, policy rates, hours, expectations and sentiment adjust with explicit delays. Financial stocks use their own flows; nominal gains in capital values are not physical investment.

4 · Demand, prices and monetary policy

Households consume fractions of disposable income plus spending from positive net liquid wealth. A specified share of new household borrowing is spent. At each date a bisection solves the goods-market equation. Prices adjust to expected inflation and capacity utilisation. The rate rule responds to inflation and utilisation. These are behavioural choices, not identities or estimated laws.

Real loan rate = (1 + nominal loan rate)/(1 + expected inflation) − 1.
Inflation = expected inflation + price response × (utilisation/normal utilisation − 1).
Policy target = anchor + inflation response × inflation gap + utilisation response × utilisation gap.

Extreme inflation, negative household deposits, invalid physical states and unsupported fiscal/funding boundaries produce explicit errors. No clipping is substituted for a broken financial account. A domain error means the selected closure is inadequate for the experiment, not that the political idea has been refuted.

5 · Labour, demographics and household wealth

Preferred hours = opening hours × real net wage ratio^(substitution elasticity − income elasticity) × aspirations / liquid-wealth leisure factor.
Required hours = max(0, essential basket − real non-wage receipts + net principal repayment)/(net hourly wage × employed workforce).
Target hours = min(explicit hours ceiling, max(preferred hours, required hours)).

The essential basket grows with population and the stated aspiration trend. Employment utilisation is min(1, utilisation/normal utilisation), a proxy rather than a labour-market equilibrium. Desired hours do not guarantee jobs; any consumption shortfall is reported. Leisure is 112 waking hours less paid work; unpaid care, commuting and household production are omitted. Net hourly wages use a common reference tax/share/hour basis across scenarios. Paid hours and household purchasing power are distinct.

Retired share follows an explicit trend within the documented domain. Pensions scale with population, retired share and prices. Old-age dependency divides retirees by participating employed working-age people. Age groups are not the model’s income classes. Worker wealth is deposits minus household borrowing plus assigned equity and capitalised net land rent. Land value = after-tax owner rent / assumed cap rate; it is not an independent land-market forecast. Public land is valued from public rent before a self-tax.

6 · Aggregate valuation and matched multiples

Sales proxy = turnover/value-added multiplier × (GDP − land rent).
EBIT = corporate EBITDA − private depreciation.
Net earnings = EBIT − business net interest − corporate tax.
FCFE = net earnings + depreciation − investment + net borrowing = dividends.
FCFF = EBIT − unlevered operating tax + depreciation − investment.
FCFE = FCFF − interest + actual tax shield + net borrowing.

Model equity discounts announced distributions at the equity required return. Values at later frames are conditional on the same future path. This gives perfect foresight about specified scenarios, not a trading forecast. EV = model equity + business net debt at carrying value; it is not a separate operating-DCF proof. P/E and P/S use the same equity numerator and matched earnings or sales. EV ratios use the same financing scope and operating denominators.

Terminal payout = 1 − g/ROE.
Terminal equity = terminal earnings × (1 + g) × terminal payout / (cost of equity − g).

Negative growth, growth at or above terminal ROE, nonpositive terminal earnings or a nonpositive discount-growth spread withhold this steady-state valuation. Terminal parameters are explicit normalised assumptions, separate from transient last-year rates. A 3×3 rate/growth sensitivity is calculated. The bond panel values a fixed ten-year coupon stream under parallel yield changes; it is a duration illustration, not a fitted yield curve.

7 · Issuer satellite

Opening sales are split into domestic-demand and foreign-growth exposures. Price pass-through, wage costs, input costs, cash rent and retained productivity affect margins. Depreciation, capex and operating working capital determine FCFF. Debt and excess cash are held constant in this satellite; interest reprices by the stated floating share. It is not a complete issuer balance-sheet forecast or a banking model.

Terminal operating value = terminal NOPAT × (1 + g) × (1 − g/ROIC)/(terminal operating discount rate − g).
Equity = operating value − debt − preferred − NCI − pensions − leases + excess cash + other non-operating assets.

The explicit forecast uses the stated operating discount rate and sensitivities to risk-free rates and ERP, not a capital-structure-derived WACC. Claims use the reporting currency. Differing quote currency requires explicit FX. Unknown claims prevent the full equity bridge and EV multiples. Cash-rent operations cannot support a nonzero capitalised lease claim without a matched adjustment, so those values are withheld. Reference quote multiples use scenario-year denominators; they are not future observed multiples.

8 · Reform ledgers and external satellites

Fisher and Kumhof transition tables show stocks, counterparties and changes in net worth; they do not supply fitted macro benefits. Fisher uses a payment bank and an investment fund. The public issuer holds fund units against its issued reserve money. The Kumhof illustration offsets bank-held public bills with Treasury credit and charges any private relief to public net worth. In the broad lab ledger, reserve money remains a public liability; interest-bearing net debt is separately labelled.

Dynamic reserve backing still accommodates bank deposit creation with public credit. Independent money growth, loan allocation, funding competition, bank capital and losses are not solved. A future full reform model must supply these closures before the plan presets can be interpreted as complete author-model results.

Fixed-MV, wheat-unit-cost and foreign-currency-debt stress calculations are separate thought experiments; they are not silently fed into the domestic macro stock-flow accounts. This prevents counting a hypothetical currency loss or cost saving twice.

9 · Data and qualification

FRED series: GDP, GCE, GPDI, NETEXP, GDPDEF, CPATAX, POPTHM and GS10. Quarterly nominal GDP, purchases, investment and net exports are in consistent SAAR units; ratios use a common quarter. The GDP deflator, corporate profits, monthly population and monthly Treasury yield supply context. The saved snapshot is latest-vintage, with retrieval and observation dates; it is not point-in-time. User CSV units are declared but not independently authenticated. Applying anchors changes three expenditure assumptions only.

The 88 macro controls and issuer inputs are synthetic until sourced. Archetype mappings and meta controls are interpretive. Numerical closure, test results and domain qualifications are in verification; original source hashes are in provenance.
